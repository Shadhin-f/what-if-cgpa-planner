Questions & answers

Does editing grades change my real result?
No. What-ifs, excluded courses and planned semesters are only saved in your browser. Nothing is ever sent to RDS3.
Is my data safe?
Yes. There's no account and no server. Your grades and plans stay in your own browser, and the extension only talks to RDS3 using your own login. Anonymous usage counts (like "a tab was opened") help improve it, and never include grades or personal details.
Why is my new tab empty or asking me to log in?
It fills in from RDS3. Log in and open your Grade History (for CGPA) and the RDS3 home page (for classes and attendance) once, then open a new tab.
I don't see the Analyze CGPA button.
It only appears on rds3.northsouth.edu/students/grade_history. Make sure you're logged in, then reload the page.
How are retakes counted?
When a course appears more than once, only the attempt with the best grade counts toward CGPA and credits. The others are marked "Excluded (retake)".
What does the × button in Edit Grades do?
It leaves that course out of your projected CGPA, along with every retake of it and its credits. The row turns gray. Press + to bring it back. "Reset all what-ifs" clears these too.
Do W and I grades count?
No. Like the official calculation, W and I don't count toward GPA or credits.
How do I change the theme or wallpaper?
Open a new tab and click the button at the bottom-right. Wallpaper and Colors are the first two groups.
Can I make the new tab simpler?
Yes. In Customize → Dashboard, turn off Full dashboard to keep just the clock, search bar and links, or hide individual sections.
My CGPA here doesn't match RDS3.
First check for what-ifs or excluded courses: the Current CGPA always uses your real grades, and Projected includes your edits. If Current still differs, please report it through the community so it can be fixed.
